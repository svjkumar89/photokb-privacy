Photo KB – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · App: Photo KB (com.axebelk.photokb) · Developer: Axebelk

Photo KB is a photo compressor that reduces photos to a target file size (for example 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB) so they can be uploaded to online forms. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Your photos stay on your device

  • Photos you select are compressed, cropped and rotated entirely on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers. We do not operate any servers for this app.
  • Compressed photos are saved to your device gallery (Pictures/Photo KB) only when you tap Save.
  • Compression history (thumbnails and file sizes) is stored only on your device. You can turn history off or clear it at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • EXIF metadata such as GPS location and camera details is removed from compressed photos.

2. Permissions

  • Photos / media: to let you pick photos and save the compressed result. Used only when you choose to.
  • Internet: used only to show advertisements.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Photo KB is free and supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob. The app requests non-personalized ads. Google may still collect and process certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal data. We have no account system, analytics or server. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above.

5. Children

Photo KB is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. Clear history in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
